# User Manual

## Getting started

Open Scamouflage and select a tab from the navigation bar. Play Career Shifts to progress through cases, or open Arcade to try individual games. New players can use the First Day tutorial to learn the controls. Sign in through the account screen to use online profile and leaderboard features.

## Arcade

| Mode | Objective | Career XP to unlock |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Trust or Bust | Investigate a post and decide whether to keep or remove it. | 0 |
| Impostor Hunt | Compare accounts and flag impersonation. | 0 |
| Scam Slam | Inspect a message and choose a safe response. | 0 |
| Inbox Sprint | Sort emails into spam or keep; tap to inspect details. | 300 |
| Comment Chaos | Flag comments that violate community rules. | 600 |
| Rumor Radar | Compare versions of a story and identify misleading changes. | 900 |
| Clip Check | Verify a video's claim using its timeline and available evidence. | 1,200 |

Endless Mix combines unlocked Arcade modes. Use the available checks before submitting an answer, and read the feedback after each round.

## Difficulty

| Difficulty | Starting hearts | Inspection budget* |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Easy | 5 | 5 |
| Medium | 4 | 4 |
| Hard | 3 | 4 |

*The shared inspection budget applies to modes that use checks. Other modes have their own controls. Medium and Hard also introduce timed email sorting in Inbox Sprint.

## Progress and settings

- Complete shifts, Arcade rounds and daily challenges to earn XP. Claim completed daily challenge rewards from the challenge screen.
- Visit Profile to view progress and equip unlocked investigator cosmetics.
- Use Ranks to view weekly and all-time leaderboards. Sign in to participate in online rankings.
- Adjust music and sound effects in Settings. Audio is optional; clues are also presented visually.
