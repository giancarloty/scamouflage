# Scamouflage original audio assets

All audio in this folder was algorithmically synthesized specifically for Scamouflage. No recordings, copyrighted melodies, or samples from other games were used.

## Audio direction
- `music/menu.mp3` and `music/arcade.mp3`: upbeat, bright electronic arcade music for navigating before play.
- Other `music/` tracks: slower, mellow investigation loops so players can read and think.
- `sfx/tap.mp3` and `sfx/choose.mp3`: short keyboard/mouse-like clicks.
- `sfx/flip.mp3`, `sfx/swipe.mp3`, `sfx/snap.mp3` and `sfx/inspect.mp3`: understated feedback intended to avoid fatigue.
- Correct, victory, and other milestone cues are retained from the previous original audio pack.

Bundled MP3s require no network connection. Master, music and effects volumes can be adjusted in Settings.
